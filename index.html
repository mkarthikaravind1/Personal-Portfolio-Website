Friday was a full build day: we completed our portfolio websites and presented a summary of the site's content and structure before the class, getting peer and mentor feedback on our work.

Week 2

Tech Talk, Coding Foundations & Design Thinking

03 Aug – 07 Aug 2026

Overview

Week 2 was the most intellectually diverse week so far, spanning a guest seminar, a personal tech talk, an introduction to computational thinking and coding, hands-on app development with MIT App Inventor, and a session on design thinking and lean startup methodology — all within five days.

Weekly Activities

Monday opened with an inspiring seminar by Prof. Mukesh Sud, followed by my own tech talk where I presented on Green Computing and the Hidden Carbon Footprint of Our Data — exploring how data centres, streaming, and AI workloads contribute to global carbon emissions and what sustainable computing practices look like. Tuesday introduced algorithmic thinking through flowcharts and the video "How to Think Like a Coder?", which reframed problem-solving as a structured decomposition process rather than jumping straight to code. Wednesday brought a practical session on Scratch, a block-based visual programming tool, before transitioning into Python fundamentals — covering variables, data types, and user input, marking the start of hands-on programming within the program. Thursday, we worked with MIT App Inventor where I developed the Hostel Warden page of a Hostel Complaint Tracker System — a mobile interface that allows wardens to view, manage, and resolve complaints submitted by students. Friday closed the week with a high-value session covering design thinking methodology, customer discovery techniques, and the Lean Startup framework, connecting the dots between user problems, rapid prototyping, and building products people actually want.

Assignments & Deliverables

Delivered a structured presentation on the environmental impact of digital infrastructure — covering data centre energy consumption, the carbon cost of video streaming and AI model training, and practical steps toward sustainable computing such as green cloud providers, efficient algorithms, and hardware lifecycle management.

Built the warden-facing module of a Hostel Complaint Tracker System using MIT App Inventor. The page allows the hostel warden to view all submitted student complaints, update their status, and mark issues as resolved — providing a simple but functional admin interface for complaint management.

Key Learnings
